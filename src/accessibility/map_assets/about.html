A hobby project, made with curiosity

A little map of Uppsala.

Hi! I’m Frans. This is a hobby project I’m building with the help of Codex to practise visualising data.

I wanted to explore how restaurants and residents are spread across Uppsala. Bringing them together on a map seemed like a fun way to learn—and to see familiar places from a different angle.

Have a look around, switch between the heat maps, and click a square to see what’s there. It’s a work in progress, and part of the fun is figuring things out along the way.

— Frans

Population and restaurant-register data come from Statistics Sweden (SCB). The background map is from OpenStreetMap contributors, displayed with Leaflet.

This is an independent hobby project. Restaurant records may be incomplete, and registered names or locations may differ from what you see on the street. Population figures refer to 2025; restaurant information comes from the latest local import.
